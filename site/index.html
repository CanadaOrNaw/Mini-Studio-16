MASTERSYNTH 1–3DRUMS

ONE PLUG • NO SOLDER • NO CUSTOM PCB

Line in and normal Bluetooth audio, in a finished Cardputer-powered cap.

The optional cap uses a retail ATOM Lite and preassembled PCM1808 module. Power and data share the one 14-pin Cardputer plug; no second cable leaves the two-part snap enclosure.

01

One outside plug

EXT 5VOUT/GND powers both modules. Cardputer battery and charging remain the only power system.

00

No board fabrication

Factory modules, precrimped/Grove leads and two hidden lever splices; no soldering or assembler order.

02

A module-retaining base and compliant snap lid expose only line input, pair button, status light and the 14-pin nose.
